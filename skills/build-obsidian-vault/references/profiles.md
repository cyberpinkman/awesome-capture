## Projects

For active work with light reference storage:

- `00 Inbox`
- `10 Active Projects`
- `20 Areas`
- `30 Resources`
- `40 Archive`
- `90 Sources`
- `99 Attachments`
- `_Templates`

Selection rules:

- Choose **general** when the user cannot yet name a dominant workflow.
- Choose **research** when claims, sources, and synthesis are primary.
- Choose **creator** when the durable output is published content.
- Choose **projects** when completion and responsibility matter more than permanent concept notes.
- For users promising less than 15 minutes of weekly upkeep, cap the non-system folder count at five.
